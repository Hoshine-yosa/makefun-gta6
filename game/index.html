SOLMAR CITY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

You are Danny Vega, a wheelman for hire in Solmar City — a sun-soaked coastal town of palm-lined boulevards, neon nights and bad decisions. Your fixer Mara Quinn has ten jobs for you: pickups, hot cars, a cross-town dash, a ghost run, a ring course, a VIP escort and one very loud getaway. Earn cash, dodge the Solmar PD, and don't get busted. Gold cash bundles and gold stars are stashed around town — grab them on foot or by car. Need quick money? Pick up a taxi fare at the cyan marker, or hit a stunt ramp at speed. Three kinds of cars roam the streets: nippy Sedans, fast Comet GT sports cars and heavy Hauler Vans — each drives differently. Mara's Garage (near the yellow marker) sells cars, guns and armor.

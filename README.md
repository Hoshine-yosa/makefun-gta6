**Solmar City** is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with three.js. You are Danny Vega, a wheelman for hire in a sun-soaked coastal city of palm-lined boulevards. Explore on foot or by car, steal cars, dodge the Solmar PD's wanted system, and run four story missions for your fixer Mara Quinn.

- **A 3D procedural city**: 36 blocks of pastel art-deco buildings, parks, parking lots, a beach, ocean and a pier — all generated from a seed.
- **On foot and by car**: third-person walking with mouse-look, sprint and jump; steal parked and traffic cars (E), arcade driving with handbrake.
- **Living streets**: AI traffic following the road grid, pedestrians who flee from danger.
- **Wanted system**: crimes raise 1–5 stars; police cruisers and officers chase, ram, shoot and arrest you; lay low to lose the heat.
- **Missions + story**: four jobs with objectives, timers and cash rewards — a pickup, a hot car, a timed delivery, and a 60-second escape from a 3-star manhunt.
- **Combat**: pistol with mouse aim, tracers, and health.
- **HUD**: cash, health, wanted stars, a live 2D minimap (player, cars, police, mission marker), speed, and mission objectives.
- **Save**: cash and completed missions persist in localStorage.
- **Sound**: fully synthesized WebAudio — engine hum, sirens, gunshots, pickup chimes and a synthwave music loop. No audio files.

No build step — just serve the game folder and open it in a browser. Everything (city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound, music) is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## Controls

- **WASD** — walk / drive · **Mouse** — look · **Click** — shoot
- **E** — steal / exit car, interact · **Shift** — sprint · **Space** — jump / handbrake
- **P** — pause · **M** — mute
